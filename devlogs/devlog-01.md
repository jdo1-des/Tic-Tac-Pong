I am intending to combine to classic games Tic Tac Toe and Pong into a bigger and better game. The game is for two players, these players will compete in rounds of pong, after the end of each round the winner will select a space on the tic tac toe board to put one of their markers. The first to have 3 markers in a row (horizontally, verticxally, or diagonally) is the grand winner.
<br>
My first step will be to make a pong game that remebers who the winner is. I will then use this to select the player for the tic tac toe part.
